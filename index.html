The rotation

Planned days are Sat · Mon · Wed, but any three days with a gap between them count. The page always shows whichever workout is next.

Rules

Three a week, A → B → C. Never two in one day, always at least one day between. Missed Monday? Do it Tuesday — the rotation just continues. Two sessions in a bad week is fine; don’t make anything up.

Start session walks you through it: a four-minute warm-up, two light sets on the first exercise, every working set with a rest countdown, then a five-minute cool-down. Or log sets by hand on the cards.

Progression. When every set reaches the top of its rep range, add one plate next time. The page tells you. Small-muscle moves (raises, curls, pushdowns) must go three reps past the top first — a plate is a big jump on a lateral raise.

Short on time? Skip the optional exercise, then the cool-down. Never the first two exercises.

After a break. Take about 10% off your last weights for the first session back, then carry on as normal. Two weeks off costs you almost nothing; restarting too heavy costs you a week of soreness.
